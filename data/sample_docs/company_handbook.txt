Acme Corp Employee Handbook
============================

Chapter 1: Welcome to Acme Corp
--------------------------------

Welcome to Acme Corp! We are a global technology company dedicated to building innovative solutions
that empower businesses worldwide. Founded in 2010, Acme Corp has grown from a small startup to
a Fortune 500 company with over 15,000 employees across 30 countries.

Our mission is to "Make complex simple" by providing enterprise-grade software that is both
powerful and easy to use. We believe that technology should amplify human potential, not replace it.

Chapter 2: Company Values
--------------------------

Our five core values guide everything we do:

1. Innovation First: We encourage bold thinking and experimentation. Every employee gets 20%
   time to work on passion projects. Our Innovation Lab has produced 12 patents in the last year.

2. Customer Obsession: We start with the customer and work backwards. Our Net Promoter Score
   of 78 reflects our commitment to customer satisfaction.

3. Radical Transparency: We share company financials, strategy, and decisions openly with all
   employees. Monthly all-hands meetings keep everyone aligned.

4. Diversity & Inclusion: We believe diverse teams build better products. Our D&I initiatives
   have increased underrepresented groups in leadership roles by 40% since 2020.

5. Sustainable Growth: We prioritize long-term value over short-term gains. Our ESG rating
   of AA reflects our commitment to environmental and social responsibility.

Chapter 6: Incident Response
------------------------------

When production incidents occur, we follow a structured response process:

Severity Levels:
- SEV1 (Critical): Complete service outage affecting all customers. Response time: 15 minutes.
- SEV2 (Major): Significant degradation affecting >25% of customers. Response time: 30 minutes.
- SEV3 (Minor): Limited impact affecting <25% of customers. Response time: 2 hours.
- SEV4 (Low): Cosmetic or minor issues. Response time: next business day.

Incident Response Process:
1. Detection: Automated monitoring alerts or customer reports trigger incident
2. Triage: On-call engineer assesses severity and pages appropriate team
3. Response: Incident commander coordinates resolution efforts
4. Communication: Status page updated, stakeholders notified
5. Resolution: Fix deployed and verified
6. Post-mortem: Blameless review within 48 hours, action items tracked to completion

On-Call Policy:
- Engineers rotate on-call weekly within their team
- On-call engineers receive $500/week additional compensation
- Maximum response time for SEV1 pages: 15 minutes
- Escalation to management if incident not acknowledged within 30 minutes
